Privacy Policy

Kääselä 2026-08-03 Last updated

The only personal data this site collects is the email address entered in the newsletter form. No other personal data is collected.

1. What we collect

The only personal information this site collects is the email address submitted through the newsletter signup form. We do not collect names, addresses, phone numbers, or similar information.

2. How we use it

The email address you provide is used only to notify you when a new Monthly Report is published. It is not used for advertising, sold to third parties, or used to contact you about anything else.

3. Third-party sharing

The signup form is handled by Netlify, Inc.'s form feature (Netlify Forms). Submitted email addresses pass through Netlify's servers before reaching the site operator. We do not sell or share this data with any other third party.

4. Cookies and analytics

This site does not use cookies. It does not use Google Analytics or any other analytics or advertising tracking tool. Google Search Console is used only by the site operator for site management and does not collect visitor personal data.

This site logs the User-Agent of AI crawlers (e.g. GPTBot, ChatGPT-User) for research purposes. This logging does not target human visitors and does not include any personally identifying information.

5. Retention and deletion

Email addresses are retained for as long as needed to send notifications. If you'd like your address removed, please email us at the address below. There is currently no automated unsubscribe mechanism, so removal requests are handled by email.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy or how your data is handled, contact kamogawa.ai.lab@gmail.com.
